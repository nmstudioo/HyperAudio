Reading guide

What these numbers mean

  • Target error is the RMS difference between raw response plus this profile and the target, in dB (IEM 30 Hz to 16 kHz, speaker 100 Hz to 16 kHz). Computed from the raw and target files, not measured acoustically.
  • Speaker profiles cap the total boost at +8 dB to protect the micro-speaker, so against the uncapped target they sit about 2 dB low on average between 100 and 2000 Hz (up to 12 dB near 500 Hz) by design.
  • Device verification: no build-specific device results are recorded for the current release. Acoustic verification: no microphone measurements have been recorded. Worst-case device CPU, speaker excursion and thermal limits also remain unverified.

Method

Every profile is a three-stage chain in RootlessJamesDSP: a VDC filter bank, a convolver impulse response and a Liveprog limiter script.

Chain order#

StageFileJob
1. VDC.vdcBiquad banks at 44.1 and 48 kHz, fitted to the same predicted curve
2. Convolver.irsConvolution: 4-channel true-stereo for IEM, 2-channel for speaker
3. Liveprog.eelInput guard, 1.5 ms lookahead limiter, true-peak guard, per-profile controls

Gain and protection#

  • Each chain is normalised to a maximum gain of -0.5 dB.
  • The limiter protects peaks; its limits are listed in Limits.

What the curves mean#

Curves on the site are calculated, not acoustic measurements. A predicted curve is the raw response plus the correction plus a constant level alignment. See Evidence for how far each claim is checked.

Sources#

  • src/profiles/<id>/ holds the artifacts.
  • src/profiles.meta.json holds the copy, stats and curves.
  • The fit library, EEL generator, target and raw data are not in the repo yet; see src/README.md.
